## Verification status definitions

| Status | Meaning | May ship? |
|---|---|---|
| `verified` | Supported by authoritative source (awarding body, certifying body, publicly documented record) with link captured below | Yes |
| `verified (self-source)` | Supported by Hero's own published material (e.g. hero-experiences.com/why-us). Acceptable for operational use; **add third-party citation before Nat Geo legal review** | Yes, with note |
| `pending` | Plausible and partially supported; specific element (year, category, exact wording) requires confirmation | Drafts only, flagged `[pending verification]` |
| `needs-source` | No evidence currently captured | No |
