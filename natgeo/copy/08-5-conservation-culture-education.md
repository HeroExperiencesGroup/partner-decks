# Slide 8.5 — Conservation, culture, education

## Mode

Immersive. Three atmospheric images, one per pillar. Magazine-spread style.

## Headline (sentence case, serif display, set across the top of the slide)

Three threads run through every expedition.

## Body (three columns; each column: image on top, pillar name in serif display below the image, paragraph in sans beneath)

### Conservation

Active partnership with the Dubai Desert Conservation Reserve. Guest expeditions contribute directly to oryx and gazelle monitoring programmes. Every visit funds the landscape that hosts it.

### Culture

Bedouin storytellers, falconers, and guides — not actors. The traditions guests encounter are still practised by the families that practise them.

### Education

Each expedition framed as field learning. Geology, ecology, navigation, astronomy, anthropology — taught by domain practitioners, not narrators.

## Image direction

Three documentary-style photographs, arranged in a magazine grid.

Conservation: a wildlife biologist or ranger working at distance with an oryx or gazelle in the reserve. Documentary, not posed. The animal is in its environment; the human is in service of the work.

Culture: a Bedouin elder telling a story by firelight, or a falconer's hands with a bird at rest. Domestic register — practice, not performance. No staged "spectacle."

Education: a guide pointing into the landscape with guests learning around, or a night-sky frame with the silhouette of an instructor identifying constellations. Atmospheric, not didactic.

All three at roughly equivalent times of day so the grid reads as one feature spread. WebP with JPG fallback. Mobile crop: stack the three columns vertically, preserving the image-headline-paragraph order.

## Layout

Three-column grid, equal column widths, generous gutters. Each column: image fills the top half of the column; pillar name set in serif display below the image; paragraph in sans beneath the pillar name. Warm-rust hairlines may separate the three columns vertically, optional.

Page metadata in small caps in the right margin.
