# Slide 7 — The solution

## Mode

Immersive. The benchmark deserves the cinematic frame. Image carries the standard; type names it.

## Headline (sentence case, serif display, set in bone over a bone band at the bottom of the slide)

A new benchmark: the National Geographic Desert Expedition — UAE.

## Body (three short lines, stacked, sans body weight, set below the headline within the bone band)

A layered expedition, not a tour

A narrative arc, not a checklist

A transformational experience, not entertainment

## Image direction

Full-bleed wide cinematic shot at dawn. Dunes rolling into haze, scale and silence, no vehicles. No figures. The landscape is the subject.

Treatment: long focal compression, warm-cool tension between dune face and morning haze. The image occupies the top two-thirds of the slide; the typography sits beneath on a bone band.

WebP with JPG fallback. Mobile crop preserves the rolling dunes across the lower two-thirds with the bone band beneath.

## Layout

Image top two-thirds, bone band bottom third. Headline in the bone band, sentence-case serif display. The three body lines stack below the headline, each a single parallel statement.

Page metadata in small caps in the right margin.

## Notes

- Headline naming convention "National Geographic Desert Expedition — UAE" is the product-naming proposal from PRD Section 8. The treatment is typographic, not a logo lockup. No badge, no monogram (PRD 3.4).
- The three body lines use the "X, not Y" structure to define the proposed standard against the commoditised category named in Slide 6.
- "Transformational" is not in the banned-adjective list (PRD 6.1); "immersive" would be banned (overused per 6.1) and is correctly absent.
- Headline word count, counting "National Geographic" as a proper-noun pair: A / new / benchmark / the / National Geographic / Desert / Expedition / UAE = 8 words. Within PRD 6.2.
- Em dash before "UAE" is grammatical (sets country code apart), not stylistic.
- No source-pack citations — the slide proposes a new product, makes no Hero credibility claim.
