# Slide 3.5 — Why the Arabian desert matters

## Mode

Immersive. Heritage and landscape are the argument. The image is the deck's first sustained statement that this is a place, not a market.

## Headline (sentence case, serif display, set in bone over the image)

The desert is older than the city around it.

## Body (short atmospheric paragraph, sans body weight, overlaid in bone on a low-contrast band along the lower third)

The Arabian desert holds 12,000 years of human cultural memory and one of the most distinctive arid ecosystems on Earth. The Bedouin tradition that mapped it is still alive. The wildlife that defined it — oryx, gazelle, sand fox — is returning. This landscape predates Dubai by millennia. It is the heritage Dubai is built on top of.

## Image direction

Full-bleed photograph. A long horizon. Sand below, sky above, low light. No human elements; no vehicles; no buildings. The composition should feel pre-human — a landscape that is its own subject.

Treatment: low-angle dawn or dusk, with cool blue and warm sand tones in tension. The horizon line sits roughly at the lower third of the frame, leaving most of the slide to sky and atmosphere. Wide aspect; the eye should travel across the frame, not into it.

WebP with JPG fallback. Mobile crop preserves a single dominant horizon line across the middle.

## Layout

Image fills the slide, edge to edge. Headline and body sit overlaid on a low-contrast band along the lower third — the band is a slight darkening of the image, not a separate fill colour. Headline above, body below, left-aligned, generous internal spacing.

Page metadata in small caps in the right margin.
