# Slide 8 — Experience design framework

## Mode

Restrained. Pillars are structured, typographic. The slide is the deck's clearest statement of values alignment with Nat Geo.

## Headline (sentence case, serif display)

Five pillars. One expedition.

## Body (five rows; each row: pillar name in serif display, detail in sans; fine warm-rust hairline rule between rows)

**Exploration** — guided by trained Explorer Guides; geology, ecology, navigation

**Wildlife and conservation** — Arabian oryx, gazelle, and desert fox tracking tied to active conservation programmes

**Cultural heritage** — Bedouin tradition and falconry as living practice, not performance

**Immersion** — silence zones and night-sky astronomy in protected reserve

**Storytelling** — each expedition framed as documentary; guests as participants, not spectators

## Footer (small, italic serif, set below the list with generous interval)

Aligned with the values Nat Geo applies across its travel partnerships: conservation, authenticity, sustainability, guest experience, and community benefit.

## Notes

- "Immersion" appears as a pillar name. PRD 6.1 bans "immersive" as a marketing adjective ("overused — earn it with imagery instead"). "Immersion" as a noun naming a structured programme component is a different use — it names a category of expedition design (silence zones, night-sky astronomy), not a marketing claim about the experience. Acceptable. If Jcamp prefers, "Solitude" or "Sky and silence" are equivalents that avoid the word entirely.
- The footer makes the values-alignment statement explicit (PRD 2.3). It is the deck's only direct enumeration of Nat Geo's travel-partnership values — kept in the footer at smaller weight so the slide reads as Hero's framework first, alignment statement second.
- "Programmes" uses British English (PRD 6.1).
- "Active conservation programmes" — the active partnership with DDCR is source pack #9 (verified). The wildlife monitoring work is publicly documented through DDCR; no additional flag required at this slide's level of generality.
- "Bedouin tradition and falconry as living practice, not performance" — the "not performance" qualifier is the deck's most direct refusal of the commoditised category named in Slide 6.
- "Explorer Guides" is capitalised as the proper name of the guide tier, not a descriptive phrase.
- Hairline rules in warm rust (#8B4A2B) per PRD 3.4.
