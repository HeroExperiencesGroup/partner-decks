# Slide 13 — Closing

## Mode

Immersive. Closes the visual loop with the cover. Full-bleed last-light image; two-column type over.

## Headline (sentence case, serif display, set in bone over the image, top of slide)

Together.

## Body (two parallel columns, set in bone over the image, mid-slide, equal weight)

| Hero Experiences brings | National Geographic brings |
|---|---|
| Proven delivery, market leadership, cultural credibility | Global authority, scientific integrity, storytelling power |

## Closing line (centred at the base of the slide, small caps, deep sand or bone)

We redefine what a desert experience means.

## Image direction

Full-bleed photograph that mirrors the cover image but at last light — the same dune ridge composition, or a near-equivalent, with the sun setting where it was rising on the cover. The visual loop is the device.

Treatment: warm low-angle final light, cooler shadows in the foreground, the sky deepening to indigo at the upper edge. No figures, no vehicles. WebP with JPG fallback.

Mobile crop preserves the dune ridge low in frame on portrait orientation, matching the cover's mobile crop.

## Layout

Image fills the slide, edge to edge. Headline ("Together.") sits at the top in bone, centred or left-aligned matching the cover's typographic position. The two columns stack mid-slide, equal weight, separated by a fine vertical rule in deep sand. Closing line sits at the base as a footer band in small caps.

Page metadata in small caps in the right margin.

Both logos (National Geographic, Hero Experiences) appear in the bottom margin at equal weight, mirroring the cover's logo treatment. No lockup, no badge.

## Notes

- "Together." — single word, with terminal period. The deck's shortest headline. The period is the cadence.
- The two-column body lists are deliberately parallel in count and cadence: three nouns each (delivery, leadership, credibility / authority, integrity, power). Do not reorder.
- "Cultural credibility" frames Hero's contribution as cultural fluency, not commercial scale.
- "Storytelling power" directly references Nat Geo's defining capability (called out in Slide 4 list).
- "We redefine what a desert experience means." — the closing line is the deck's last sentence. The first-person plural matches Slide 12.5 ("We are asking…"), closing the loop on the explicit ask.
- The visual mirror with Slide 1 (cover image at first light → closing image at last light) is the deck's structural device. Phase B1 must select cover and closing images as a pair, not independently.
- No source-pack citation required — the slide is the closing rhetorical statement, not a factual claim about Hero.
