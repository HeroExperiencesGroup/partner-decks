## Notes

- "Ultra-premium experiential travel" — "experiential" is not in the banned-adjective list (PRD 6.1). "Ultra-premium" is descriptive of the category, not a self-marketing claim about Hero.
- The right-arrow glyph (→) in "UAE → Saudi Arabia → arid landscapes worldwide" is a typographic glyph used to mark sequence. It is not a decorative icon (PRD 3.4 prohibits icons; this is a directional glyph in the same family as a colon). Acceptable.
- "Content pipeline: documentary, digital, editorial, education" covers PRD 2.5 original-brief items "Global branding opportunity" and "Media/content/storytelling opportunity."
- The list moves from immediate (entry into the GCC), to flagship (in a top tourism hub), to scalable (worldwide). The order is the argument.
- No Hero credibility claims; no source-pack citation required at the list level.
