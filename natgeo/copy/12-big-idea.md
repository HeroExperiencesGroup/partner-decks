# Slide 12 — The big idea

## Mode

Immersive. The deck's emotional peak. Full-bleed dark image; pull quote in bone, centred.

Treatment: cinematic, almost black-and-white. The quote sits in bone over the lower-mid section, where the image is darkest.

WebP with JPG fallback. The dark image weight is acceptable here; the PRD 11.5 budget is for the full deck (under 15MB), not per slide.
