# Slide 3 — The vision

## What's in it

- Headline (serif display, overlaid bottom-left in bone): *One expedition. Two access levels. One ethos.*
- Body (two-line caption format below the headline, sans, lighter weight):
  > A co-created experience between National Geographic and Hero Experiences Group — delivered through the Heritage Collection (documentary, communal, cultural) and the Platinum Collection (private, 1:2-staffed, Michelin-curated). Both inside the UAE's first national park. Both grounded in Bedouin tradition. Both worthy of the Arabian desert.

## Image

Full-bleed wide landscape at golden hour. Scale and silence — long horizon, dunes rolling into haze. No vehicles (forward-partnership slide). No people. No buildings. Aspect: cinematic wide. Lighting: low warm light, atmospheric dust catching the sun. The colour is from place — bone, deep sand, warm rust — not from any brand palette.

## Feel

The first emotional beat after the paradox lands. The image gives the reader permission to *want* what comes next. The headline ("One expedition. Two access levels. One ethos.") is the deck's whole pitch in eight words, planted early so every later slide reads in its frame.
