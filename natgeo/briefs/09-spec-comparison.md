# Slide 9 — Hero vs typical Dubai operator

## What's in it

- Headline (serif display, left-aligned, generous top margin): *Where the difference lives.*
- Body — three-column spec table. Left column (small-caps sans, deep sand) = attribute. Middle column (sans, ink) = typical Dubai operator. Right column (sans, ink) = Hero. Fine warm-rust hairline rules between rows.

  | Attribute | Typical Dubai operator | Hero |
  |---|---|---|
  | Terrain access | Public desert | DDCR — UAE's first national park; Royal Family private-property access within the reserve |
  | Driving practice | Dune-bashing | Gentle-drive policy — only operator in Dubai to refuse dune-bashing |

  | Animal welfare | Variable | Compliant with Global Welfare Guidance for Animals in Tourism |
  | Sustainability certification | Variable | Ecotourism-certified; Dubai Sustainable Tourism + Dubai Green Tourism |
  | Plastic discipline | Variable | 200,000 single-use bottles displaced annually |

- Closing line (smaller sans, below the table, centred or left-aligned): *Editorial luxury through fact. Conservation through operating practice.*
