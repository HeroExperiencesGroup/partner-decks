## Image

Full-bleed, a single dune ridge at first light. No people. No vehicles. No buildings. Sky is just beginning to colour; the dune face holds shadow detail. Documentary register — the photograph that opens a Nat Geo feature, not the photograph that sells a hotel. Aspect: landscape, generous sky. Texture: ripple lines, soft grain, no digital sharpening.

## Feel

The reader opens to silence. No claim, no movement, no logo shouting. They are held for a beat before the typography registers. By the time the eye finds the title, the deck has already established its register — this is a place serious enough to deserve a serious proposal.
