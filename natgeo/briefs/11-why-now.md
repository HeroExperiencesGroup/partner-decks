# Slide 11 — Why now

## What's in it

- Headline (serif display, left-aligned, generous top margin): *This is the window.*
- Body — four short lines, stacked, generous interval, sans:
  - The GCC is becoming the world's fastest-growing premium tourism region
  - Conservation-led travel is replacing volume-driven travel
  - Saudi Arabia and the UAE are scaling cultural and natural destinations at unprecedented pace
  - The category leader will be defined now, not later
- Closing line (smaller sans, below, left-aligned): *A definitive desert expedition can be claimed once. The moment is open.*

## Image

Default: none. Bone background.

Variant: a single restrained landscape image as a thin horizon strip at the very base of the slide. Wide aspect, very short height. No figures, no vehicles, no buildings. Quiet — not a hero image. Implement as CSS class toggle matching the Slide 2 mechanism.

## Feel

The deck's urgency beat. Quiet, declarative, no exclamation — the rhetorical force is in the parallel structure of the four lines. Reader walks away with a sense that the decision isn't open indefinitely without feeling pressured. The closing line lands as observation, not as sales pressure.
