# Slide 2 — The paradox

## What's in it

- Headline (serif display, left-aligned, generous top margin): *Conservation and luxury are sold as opposites.*
- Body — two stacked statements, generous interval between, mid-weight sans:
  - *Conservation travel asks the guest to give something up.*
  - *Luxury travel asks the landscape to give something up.*
- Closing line (smaller sans, bottom): *The Arabian desert deserves an expedition that refuses the trade-off.*
- Optional sparse image in the right third — empty dune horizon at last light

## Image

Default: no image. Bone background. Typography alone carries the slide.

Alternative variant (if Jcamp prefers): a single restrained landscape in the right third — empty dune horizon, no people, no vehicles, last light. Aspect: tall, about 2:3. Low saturation. The image is a margin, not a subject.

## Feel

The deck announces its argument plainly. No image (or only a margin image), no atmosphere — the words have to land on their own. Reader sits with the tension for a moment before moving on. The whitespace is doing as much work as the type. This is the load-bearing slide of the deck — every later slide is downstream of the paradox stated here.
