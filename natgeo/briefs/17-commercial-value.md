# Slide 17 — Commercial and brand value for Nat Geo

## What's in it

- Headline (serif display, left-aligned, generous top margin): *What this opens for Nat Geo.*
- Body — five-line list, generous spacing, sans:
  - Entry into ultra-premium experiential travel in the GCC at two access tiers — broadening the addressable audience
  - A flagship product family in one of the world's top tourism hubs
  - A content pipeline: documentary, digital, editorial, education — drawn from a full range of experience types
  - A scalable model: UAE → Saudi Arabia → arid landscapes worldwide
  - A licensing/royalty model that compounds across tiers, not a single SKU

None. Bone background.

## Feel

The deck's commercial argument, made in plain declarative voice. Reader registers: this isn't a one-off product; it's a product *family* with scaling logic. The fifth bullet is the v5-specific framing — tier range as commercial upside. No charts, no figures, no bar graphs — the strategic argument lands on the structure of the language alone.
