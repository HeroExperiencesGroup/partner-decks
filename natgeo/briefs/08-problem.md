## Feel

The deck's first head-to-head moment. Not a critique of any specific operator — a documentary statement of category vs operating standard. The reader sees the difference before reading any words. The captions specify; the images persuade. Three tabs argue three *dimensions* of the same contrast: how it feels, how it operates, where it lives. Tabs deepen the comparison without overloading any single slide — direct response to higher-up feedback that v4 implied differentiation without proving it.
