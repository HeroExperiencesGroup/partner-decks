# Slide 19 — The big idea

## What's in it

- No headline.
- Pull quote (large serif italic, centred, bone-coloured over dark image, full visual weight, two lines):
  > Dubai does not need another desert safari.
  > It needs the one the world recognises as the best.

## Image

Full-bleed, very dark — a desert at blue hour, almost monochrome. The image is texture and tone, not subject. Quote sits in bone over the lower-mid section, with sufficient contrast for readability. No figures, no vehicles per §6.5 (forward-partnership slide). Sky takes up two-thirds of the frame. Sand reads almost as silhouette.

## Feel

The deck's emotional peak. Single pull quote, no body copy, no list. Reader stops moving. Two lines, parallel structure — the first refuses ("does not need"), the second claims ("needs the one"). The dark image holds attention without competing for it. This is the slide that gets quoted in the follow-up email.
