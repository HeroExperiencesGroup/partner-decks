# Slide 16 — What makes this unmatched

## What's in it

- Headline (serif display, sentence case, centred, generous top margin): *Not replicable.*
- Body — two columns, equal weight, divided by a fine vertical hairline rule in deep sand running the full body height. Each column carries a serif-display header and a one-line sans descriptor:
  - **Nat Geo** — global authority
  - **Hero** — local operational mastery, across both access tiers
- Pull line (italic serif, centred below both columns, clear top margin): *Science. Story. Access. Execution.*
- Closing line (smaller sans, centred, below the pull line, generous interval): *The only desert expedition chosen not by default, but because it stands above everything else.*

## Image

None. Typographic.

## Feel

The structural argument of the partnership, made in two columns. Reader sees the complementarity at a glance. The pull line "Science. Story. Access. Execution." compresses both contributions into four nouns — Nat Geo brings Science and Story, Hero brings Access and Execution. The structure is intentional; do not reorder. Closing line refuses "exclusive" framing while making the same argument.

## Notes

- Hero descriptor gains "across both access tiers" in v5 — small change that connects this slide to the deck's new spine.
- "Chosen not by default, but because it stands above everything else" — the rhetorical claim is that the expedition succeeds on merit, not on scarcity. The line avoids "exclusive" (banned per PRD §6.1, except in factual constructions — which this is not).
