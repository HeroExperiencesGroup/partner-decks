# Slide 10 — The two collections

## What's in it

- Headline (serif display, across both columns at the top): *Two access levels. One ethos.*
- Body — two parallel columns under each photograph, equal weight, each carrying three stacked lines:

  | Heritage Collection | Platinum Collection |
  |---|---|
  | Documentary, communal, cultural | Private, 1:2-staffed, Michelin-curated |
  | Bedouin camp, shared table | Private oasis, fire-lit, single party |
  | From AED 595 | From AED 1,950 |

- Closing line (italic serif, centred below both columns, clear top margin): *Same reserve. Same guides. Same animal-welfare rules. Different access.*
- A thin centreline rule in deep sand, full slide height, between the two photographs.

## Image

Two photographs side by side at equivalent dusk light. Both documentary, not advertising-lit. Equal weight.

- *Left (Heritage):* a torch-lit communal camp at dusk; a Bedouin host telling a story by a low fire; guests seated on cushions at a long shared table. The camp is sparse — woven mats, low cushions, woodsmoke. Vintage Land Rover permitted at the edge of the frame in soft focus (per §6.5, current-operations vehicle imagery is allowed on this slide).
- *Right (Platinum):* a private cabana at a fire-lit oasis; antique brass lamps; a small table set for two; dunes beyond, just losing light. No one in frame, or a single host figure seen from middle distance. No marble. No champagne flutes. No gold leaf. Documentary register — would appear in a Nat Geo Traveler feature on Singita, not in a Burj Al Arab brochure.

## Feel

This is the slide that resolves the deck's central thesis. The reader sees the range — two atmospheres, side by side, equal weight. Neither is dressed up to outshine the other; the closing line names what they share. The Platinum image must communicate luxury through restraint (fire, lamp, sparse table) not through signifiers (gold, marble, glass). The reader walks away understanding that Hero is one operator with two access levels, not a luxury brand and a budget brand.

## Notes
